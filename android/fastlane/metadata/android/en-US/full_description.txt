Homelydrive is a free Google Drive alternative that runs on your own PC. The Android app connects to your Homelydrive server through Tailscale – no port forwarding, no third-party cloud.

<b>Upload folders</b>
<ul>
<li>Choose any folders of your phone: camera, messenger pictures, downloads, documents …</li>
<li>New files are uploaded right away, also in the background and after a reboot</li>
<li>Each folder gets its own target folder on the server</li>
<li>Upload only: files deleted on the phone stay on the server</li>
<li>Optional: delete files on the phone after the upload to free space</li>
<li>Large files without limit, resumes after connection drops, recognises duplicates</li>
</ul>

<b>More</b>
<ul>
<li>Browse, search and share all files (the server's web interface)</li>
<li>Server files in the Android "Files" app and in every file picker</li>
<li>"Share" from any app: upload to Homelydrive</li>
<li>Document scanner: photograph pages and upload them as a PDF</li>
<li>Widget with the upload status</li>
<li>Notifications about new shares</li>
</ul>

The app uses no Google services and no trackers. You need your own Homelydrive server (Windows or Linux) and Tailscale.
